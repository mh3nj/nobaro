# Support

This is a one-person project, so support is best-effort.

## Bug Reports & Feature Requests

Open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/your-username/nobaro/issues) and I'll get to it when I can.

## Before You Ask

- Check existing issues — someone might already be working on it.
- Be clear about what you're seeing vs. what you expect.

## Response Times

I can't promise a timeline. I'll do my best to reply within a few days, but sometimes life gets in the way. Thanks for understanding.
